AI product features

Answers from your own data, actions the model can call safely, and a record of every turn.

Retrieval-augmented generation that runs in production, not in a notebook. I have built it twice: at Parallel with LangChain against OpenAI and Anthropic on pgvector and Pinecone, and in Saidic with pgvector and Postgres full-text search, a decision model for routing and reply checks, and a trace of what every answer read and cost. When the model has to act, the credentials are encrypted, the outbound calls are guarded against reaching your internal network, and nothing a page says is ever treated as an instruction.

You get

  • Ingestion from your sources into chunks and embeddings, with the re-indexing that happens when the source changes
  • Vector search on pgvector where your data already lives in Postgres, or Pinecone where it does not, plus hybrid full-text
  • Tool calls against your systems with encrypted credentials, OAuth flows handled, and SSRF-safe outbound requests
  • A record per turn of what was retrieved, what was checked, how long it took and what it cost
  • The same deploy as everything else: a container, a pipeline, Terraform

See it working

  • Saidic RAG over a business's knowledge, actions against its APIs, every turn traced.
  • Parallel Production RAG and recommendation services on the same AWS platform as everything else.

I did exactly this at Parallel, from no pipeline to one that handles every service, and I still own it.
