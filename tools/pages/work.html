Work

What I did at each job, then the things I built on my own time, then every capability and where it was proven.

jobs

  • Saidic2026–present Founder & Engineer. An AI assistant for business websites: it answers only from the business's own knowledge, acts on its APIs with the customer's consent, and hands over to a human from a live dashboard. FastAPI, PostgreSQL with pgvector, Redis workers, two Next.js apps.
  • Parallel2023–26 Senior Backend & Platform Engineer, remote. Owned the AWS infrastructure with Terraform IaC, led performance and security work on the highest-traffic APIs, built production RAG, and shipped the services and front-end features around them.
  • MTripleP2022–23 ML-driven image and video analysis. Lead engineer on the Django and FastAPI APIs for colour detection and media feature extraction, and the pipelines behind them.
  • Nova2018–22 Client platforms end to end. Four years of REST APIs in Django and Flask, and the interactive front ends in Vue on top of them.

open source and side projects

Built on my own time, most of it in Go. Each one ships as something you can install and run. All projects.

  • AgentFlowlive 2026 A background daemon that runs Claude Code and OpenCode in real PTYs on your computer and lets you drive them from your phone, over a Cloudflare tunnel or your own Tailscale. Go · Next.js · systemd / launchd · cosign-signed releases
  • Scoutdemo 2026 A Slack hiring agent on Trigger.dev. The approval step is a tool with no execute function, so the run suspends until a human says yes; the safety rules live in Postgres and code, not the prompt. TypeScript · Trigger.dev · Postgres
  • Pennywiselive 2026 Local-first expense tracking. One Go binary, one SQLite file, installs itself as an OS service. Optional Telegram bot with your own LLM key. Go · SQLite · Telegram · BYOK LLM
  • Job Nomad2026 Hybrid job search: full-text, pgvector embeddings and a cross-encoder rerank, with a ranking-quality gate in CI and quantised local inference. Go · Python · pgvector · ONNX Runtime
